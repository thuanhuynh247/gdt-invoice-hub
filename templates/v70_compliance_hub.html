{% call glass_card('Decree 06/2022/NĐ-CP Compliance & Quota Guide', 'bi bi-book', 'text-success', 'Comprehensive regulatory checklist and legal workflows for businesses importing or manufacturing controlled substances in Vietnam.') %}
Step 1: ODS Quota & Use Registration

Under Article 24 of Decree 06/2022/NĐ-CP, all organizations importing, exporting, or manufacturing controlled substances (ODS) must register their annual demand with the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment (MONRE).

  • Filing Deadline: Before December 31 of the preceding year.
  • Required Documentation:
    • Form 01 (Register of controlled substance demand).
    • Business license & import track record.
    • Detailed chemical recycling or disposal plan if applicable.
  • Key Tip: Scientific research institutions and medical device manufactures must attach product certifications to qualify for the 100% licensing fee exemptions.
Step 2: Customs Reconciliation & HS Codes

Once the quota is allocated, every shipment must undergo strict HS Code verification during customs declaration to ensure total volume remains within the licensed ceiling.

ODS Group Key HS Codes ODP Factor Licensing Authority
CFC 2903.77.xx 1.0 MONRE / Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT)
HCFC 2903.71.00, 2903.72.00 0.055 MONRE / Customs Department
HFC 2903.39.90, 3824.78.00 0.000 Automatic clearance (Subject to GWP monitoring)
Halon 2903.76.00 10.0 Strict quota control (Fire prevention exemption only)
Step 3: Annual ODS Consumption Report

All licensed ODS importers and manufacturers are legally obligated to submit a comprehensive compliance and usage report detailing their actual consumption, recycling rate, and stockpile volumes.

Hard Deadline: January 15 of the following year. Late filings automatically suspend import licenses for the subsequent cycle.
  • Reports must match exactly with the purchase invoices and customs XML logs parsed in GDT Invoice Hub.
  • Unused quotas cannot be carried over to the next year.
  • Any physical leakages or warehouse losses must be reported with official environmental inspector signatures.
Administrative Violations & Penalties

Non-compliance with ODS regulations triggers heavy administrative penalties and immediate quota suspensions under Decree 155/2016/NĐ-CP and Decree 45/2022/NĐ-CP.

Quota Overruns & Unlicensed Imports

Importing controlled substances without a license or exceeding the annual quota ceiling is fined between 100,000,000 VND and 250,000,000 VND, alongside mandatory re-export or destruction of the illegal shipment.

Reporting Failures & Stockpile Mismatches

Failing to report annual usage or providing inaccurate inventory records triggers fines between 30,000,000 VND and 50,000,000 VND, with automatic demotion of taxpayer compliance tier in GDT risk index.
